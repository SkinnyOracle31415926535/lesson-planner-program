# Future announcement chooser

The daily Planner deliberately has no Update Inbox or Codex Intake surface.

A future private-only announcement chooser may present coach-approved options
from explicitly authorized email and weekly-note sources. That work must define
the source permissions, review step, retention rules, and owner approval before
any account connection, mailbox access, crawl, calendar integration, or vault
write is added. No such connection is included in this release.
